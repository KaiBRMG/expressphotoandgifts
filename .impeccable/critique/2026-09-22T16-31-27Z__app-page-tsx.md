## Overall Impression

Two builds in this repo. One is accomplished: a cover wall at non-harmonic 58/74/46s so it never falls into phase, a 98deg scrim that solves the white-studio-ground problem, a catalogue arc whose sixteen frames re-seat off a single inherited custom property. ## What's Working

1. **The cover wall is load-bearing craft.** Three rows at 58/74/46s, alternate rows reversed, each holding its set twice so -50% seams invisibly, plus counter-running strips at 66/52s. The left-weighted 98deg gradient holds the type side dark enough for white display type and releases to 0.08 on the right so products keep their true colour.
2. **The catalogue wheel is real geometry with correct progressive enhancement.** `--i = --n - --mid + --spin`, drop `--k x i^2`, lean `--step x i`. Motion writes ONE custom property and sixteen frames re-seat. `--spin: 0` is a complete static default; reduced motion flattens to a hand-scrollable rail.
3. **data.ts is disciplined about truth.** Every image carries alt text for someone who cannot see it. Spec diagrams excluded by rule. No price, review, rating or turnaround invented.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The page's only standing action does nothing

`OrderDock.tsx:36` renders `<button type="button" aria-disabled="true" data-href="/shop">Shop now</button>` - no onClick, no href, nothing reads data-href. `position: fixed`, visible from first paint at every width, loudest object on every screen.

**Why it matters:** this is the entire conversion funnel on a Persuade surface. Also an a11y contradiction - aria-disabled announces "unavailable" while CSS renders it fully enabled with hover lift and glow.

**Fix:** make it the real action it can be today - `<a href={whatsapp("Hi! I'd like to start an order for ...")}>Start an order</a>`.

**Command:** /impeccable harden

### [P0] The sign-up fabricates an outcome and discards a real email address

`Newsletter.tsx:42` is `preventDefault(); setDone(true)` - no endpoint, nothing stored. It displays "Thanks - we'll be in touch" beneath a consent line stating "By entering your email you agree to receive email marketing from Express Photo and Gifts", with `autoComplete="email"`.

**Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md principle 5 forbids exactly this shape, and it carries legal weight - it collects PII under a stated marketing consent and throws it away.

**Fix:** post it somewhere real, or replace with an explicit placeholder naming what the owner must supply.

**Command:** /impeccable harden

### [P0] Roughly twenty links lead to routes that do not exist

4 service cards -> `/shop/${slug}`; 16 carousel frames -> `/shop`; catalogue link -> `/shop`; plus `href="#"` on header "Shop now", Search, Account, Cart, gift-wrapping. `app/shop` does not exist.

**Why it matters:** under Persuade the whole page is browse-bait and every browse action dead-ends on an unbranded 404 with no way back.

**Fix:** point service cards at `whatsapp(service.ask)` - the four prefills are already in data.ts and imported by nothing. Delete Search/Account/Cart.

**Command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P1] The copy belongs to a fictional competitor while 103 real products sit unused

**Fix:** rewrite every above-fold line from PRODUCT.md and product_list.json. Put Scottburgh and "collect at the counter" in the h1 or lede, not line 297. Put at least one real price and one real measurement on the page.

**Command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P1] Shop credibility is last, outside `<main>`, and its name is set in a borrowed font

`</main>` closes at page.tsx:313; address, hours, five channels and `id="find-us"` are all inside `<footer>`. The header's "Find Us" points OUT of the main landmark. The footer sets "Express" at 20vw in Montserrat italic - a third typeface absent from DESIGN.md's two-family system, and not the supplied logo in /public/logo/.

**Fix:** move find-us into `<main>`. Add a live open/closed read off HOURS. Set the wordmark from the supplied logo or in Archivo.

**Command:** /impeccable layout
