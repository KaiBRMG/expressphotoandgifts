# Express Photo and Gifts

E-commerce website for a photo printing and gift shop. See `PRODUCT.md` for users, positioning and stack decisions, and `DESIGN.md` for the design system (colours, type, components).

## Current scope

- The homepage and the shop exist: `/shop` (storefront) and `/shop/<slug>` (catalogue for `all`, `specials` or a category, with rail, type chips, occasion and recipient filters, sort, price band and photo filter). Product pages exist at `/shop/product/<slug>` (`app/shop/product/`), where Add to cart fills the cart (the page's only order route; WhatsApp there is only for an unpriced variant or asking about a date) and a photo-needs block shows the photo count, print area and shape from `photoSpec` before the customer commits. The cart is client-side (`app/site/cartStore.ts`, localStorage; line logic in `lib/catalog/cart.ts`): once it holds anything, the dock's gold pill becomes Cart with the unit count and opens the cart drawer (`app/site/Cart.tsx`), which the header's cart mark (`HeaderCart`, with the count on a gold disc) opens at any count, which re-checks prices against `/api/prices` and goes on to checkout: the photo step, or straight to details when nothing takes a photo. The photo step of checkout exists at `/checkout` (`app/checkout/`): every cart line that takes photos is drawn as its slots (count, print area and shape from `photoSpec`), beside one tray of uploads; a photo can fill any number of slots, and each slot has its own edit (crop, zoom, quarter turns, greyscale, a print turned on its side; `lib/catalog/checkout.ts`), and nothing judges a photo's sharpness (removed on purpose: that is the shop's to check); zooming out past filling shows the whole photo with white borders. Frames say the print is not included and offer the matching print (here and in the cart drawer, via `/api/needs`). Uploads go from the browser to Cloudinary under `orders/<ref>/`, signed by `/api/uploads/sign` one file per signature (the signature names the file) and rate-limited per IP (`lib/rateLimit.ts`). The photo step makes the ref with a secret key kept in localStorage; the first order saved under a ref claims it with the key's hash, and only that key can replace the unpaid order. An unpaid checkout starts over in the browser after 10 days, and a daily cron (`vercel.json` → `/api/cron/sweep-uploads`, `lib/checkout/uploads.ts`) deletes the photos of orders never paid after 14. A photo is placed whole and untouched by default (never auto-cropped), and an untouched place sends the original upload; a place the customer edits is rendered in the browser at full resolution and uploaded as its own file (`bake` in `app/checkout/images.ts`). The photo step goes on to `/checkout/details` (`app/checkout/details/`): contact, collect-or-courier (The Courier Guy, `lib/checkout/courier.ts`: quoted for the parcels the cart packs into (`lib/checkout/packing.ts`: each line's size and weight times its quantity, counted into rows, columns and layers of the smallest of the three boxes in `lib/checkout/boxes.ts` that holds it, more than one parcel when it doesn't fit, own packaging at its own size when no box does, the standard box at least when something has no measurements), the boxes' names, outside sizes and empty weights set in the admin's Settings (`settings/shop.boxes`, placeholders until saved), and each product's `shipping` (length, width, height, weight, packed; `lib/catalog/measures.ts`, overridable per variant), set in the editor's Checkout tab, where a measure worked out from the description and photos rather than measured is marked Estimated until staff type or confirm it; the order's value is declared up to the free cover, R1,000, and above it the customer may tick to insure the full value, capped at TCG's R30,000; an optional gift recipient's name and mobile become TCG's delivery contact), a review with the placed photos, and payment by PayFast (`lib/checkout/payfast.ts`: Onsite window with the redirect form as fallback). Placing an order (`placeOrder`) re-prices everything on the server (orders under R5, PayFast's floor, are refused), writes `orders/{ref}` as pending and freezes it as a payment attempt (`orders/{ref}/attempts/{ref}-{n}`, PayFast's `m_payment_id`), so a payment always pays for exactly what was on screen when Pay was pressed (`lib/checkout/orders.ts`). Only the PayFast notice at `/api/payfast/notify`, after its four checks (signature with the required passphrase, PayFast's published IP ranges or hosts, amount to the cent against its attempt, and PayFast's validate), marks it paid. Every notice is recorded under `orders/{ref}/notices`; a second payment, a wrong amount or a signed notice that failed a check flags the order (`flagged`, `issues`) for the shop, and an unsigned one is never written. Once paid, the shop and the customer are emailed through Resend (`lib/checkout/paid.ts`, `lib/checkout/emails.ts`, `lib/email.ts`). A "Save my details for next time" tick under the contact fields adds a password field beside the email; paying then makes the account (before the pending order is saved, so the order carries it) and signs the customer in. The receipt is `/checkout/done?ref=&t=`, which every customer email links to as "Track my order": it tracks the order as one line of steps, the shop's stages (timed by `stagedAt`) joined to The Courier Guy's events once booked (`lib/checkout/tracking.ts`), redraws every minute while open, and asks TCG again after the page is sent when the stored status is over 10 minutes old (once per order per window). When the order has no account, it offers one (`lib/account/auth.ts`, session helpers shared with the admin in `lib/identity.ts`); `/account` (`app/account/`, `lib/account/orders.ts`) signs in; signed in, it tracks every order still on its way with the receipt's tracking card, lists every paid order on the account (`orders/{ref}.uid`) by the customer's own placed photos with its receipt and Order again, and holds the saved details (edited in place), one marketing switch (`marketing/{uid}`: the first agreement's date kept, every change after it added to `changes`), Change password (other devices signed out) and Delete account (password to confirm; refused while an order is on its way; deletes the sign-in, `customers/{uid}`, `marketing/{uid}` and every order's photos, and keeps the orders unlinked as sales records). Order again puts the same lines in the cart at today's prices and stock, with the old placed files back in the photo step (`photos.reuse`); `placeOrder` accepts a file from outside the order's folder only when it was placed on a paid order on the same account (`reusableFiles`). Orders placed with the email before the account join it only once a link emailed to that address proves it (`/account/verify`, a hashed one-day token in `customers/{uid}.emailCheck`); after that, guest orders with the email join on each visit. Checkout no longer sends orders on WhatsApp. Settings live in `.env.local`: `FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_KEY`, `FIREBASE_WEB_API_KEY`, `ADMIN_EMAILS`, `SITE_URL`, `PAYFAST_MERCHANT_ID`, `PAYFAST_MERCHANT_KEY`, `PAYFAST_PASSPHRASE`, `PAYFAST_SANDBOX`, `PAYFAST_ONSITE`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`, `ORDER_EMAIL_TO` (only the default until Settings saves who the shop's new-order email goes to, `settings/shop`, `lib/settings.ts`), `TCG_API_KEY`, `TCG_API_URL` (production, `https://api.portal.thecourierguy.co.za`, when unset; the sandbox is `https://api.shiplogic.com` with a sandbox account's key), `TCG_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `CRON_SECRET` (the crons refuse to run without it), `VERCEL_API_TOKEN`, `VERCEL_PROJECT_ID` (set by Vercel on its own deployments), `VERCEL_TEAM_ID` (the Analytics page's traffic).
- Stock is per variant (SKU), in `stock/{sku}` with every change under `moves` (`lib/stock.ts`; the rules shared with the browser in `lib/catalog/stock.ts`). A SKU is untracked, and always orderable, until staff switch it on at `/admin/stock` (`app/admin/(console)/stock/`, `lib/admin/stock.ts`: receive a delivery, set a count, which is refused if the count moved since the page loaded, and set Sold out at, where the size shows Sold out on the shop, and Low at, which warns). Two caches, so a sale doesn't re-render the shop: pages read only which SKUs are sold out (`getSoldOut`, tag `stock-sold-out`, cleared only when a SKU goes in or out of sold out) for Sold out sizes, cards and schema.org availability, and the counts (`getAvailability`, tag `stock`) reach the browser only through `/api/stock`, which the cart and the product page fetch; asking for more than there is shows the most available now with "Order N" or "Ask on WhatsApp" (`app/site/StockLimit.tsx`). Every count change goes through `writeLevel` and clears the caches itself (`stockChanged`). `placeOrder` checks the live counts; paying takes the units off inside `applyNotice`'s transaction (kept on the order as `stock`), flags an oversold order, and emails the new-order recipients once when a SKU first reaches Low (`lowStockEmail`). A paid order's page has Put back in stock (`returnStock`, once), for a refund or cancellation however it was made. The dashboard has a Running low card.
- Campaigns (`/admin/campaigns`, `app/admin/campaigns/`, storage `lib/admin/campaigns.ts`) are the shop's deals: each holds one or more rules written as sentences (price off: percent, rand or a sale price; buy X get Y; buy this and that is reduced; spend a threshold and save), aimed at chosen products and sizes, categories, tags or everything, with dates in SAST, caps (orders, units, once per customer by email hash under `campaigns/{id}/customers`), a storefront feature and a log. One engine, `lib/catalog/campaigns.ts`, prices them in the browser and on the server: everything stacks, price deals first (the price shown on the shop), then item deals, then spend thresholds on what is left, never below R0. Cart lines keep the catalogue price; `/api/deals` (static, five minutes) gives the cart the live and soon-starting campaigns, whose dates the browser checks itself; `placeOrder` reads them fresh (`lib/campaigns.ts`), each order line keeps its catalogue `base` price and, when item deals or a spend threshold cut it further, `paid` (what refunding that line alone gives back; the admin's print sheet shows it), and the order its `discount` and `savings`; the engine breaks ties by SKU, never cart order, so browser and server agree, and when they still differ `placeOrder` returns its own total, which checkout shows and the next Pay pays, and paying counts each campaign's use in the same transaction. The Specials shelf, the card and product-page deal words and the storefront's specials band all come from live campaigns (the old hand-set `special` flag is gone; products still carrying it are offered as a draft campaign). Shop pages rebuild every five minutes so starts and ends land on time. The editor warns, never blocks (`campaignWarnings`), on deals that make something free or take over half off, a sale price that changes nothing, a spend saving over half the spend, shop wording naming amounts the deal doesn't give, sold-out and low stock on either side of a deal, a unit cap above stock, and stacked prices reaching R0 or under PayFast's floor; the low-stock email and the dashboard name the live campaign selling a low size.
- The admin's Customers page (`/admin/customers`, `app/admin/(console)/customers/`, data in `lib/admin/customers.ts`) is read-only and read fresh: one customer per email, every paid order joined under it (an order placed signed in counts under its account's email) with the account, if any; accounts with no order (the footer's sign-up) show only under Marketing email. Views All, Regulars (2+ paid orders, by spend) and Marketing email, whose opted-in list downloads as CSV (`/admin/customers/export`). A customer's page (`/admin/customers/<key>`, the `customerKey` email hash, so no email in the URL; any other email their paid orders used finds them too) shows contact (call, WhatsApp, email), every order including unpaid ones, what they buy, deals used, courier addresses and gift recipients, and the marketing consent history (`allConsent` in `lib/account/auth.ts`). A paid order's glance card links to its customer.
- The admin's Courier page (`/admin/courier`, `lib/admin/courier.ts`) is TCG's own record of a month: every waybill on the account, matched to its order by reference, with TCG's net charge from its billing transactions against what the customer paid for delivery; waybills from no order, extra waybills, cancelled-but-charged and over-cost ones come first. `/admin/courier/statement?month=` redirects to TCG's statement PDF.
- The admin's Analytics page (`/admin/analytics?month=`, `app/admin/(console)/analytics/`, data in `lib/admin/analytics.ts`) is the owner's month, read fresh: Takings on the one blue ground (net of refunds as the headline, cash basis in SAST: a sale on its `paidAt`, a refund on its `at`, found among orders whose `updatedAt` moved since the month before; the pace of the month against the last, day by day; and a ledger from catalogue prices through deals, delivery and refunds to what was kept after PayFast's fees, from its history, and TCG's charges, from its billing, either shown as not read rather than guessed), then what sold (categories, products, sizes, campaigns from `savings`), how far customers got, who ordered, how long orders took (`stagedAt`, TCG's delivered event) and Vercel Web Analytics. The funnel's browser steps (product view, add to cart, photo step, details) and searches that found nothing are the shop's own counters: `track()` (`app/site/track.ts`, once a visit per step through `sessionStorage`) beacons to `/api/track`, which drops other sites, crawlers and unlisted products, is rate-limited per IP and adds to `analytics/{YYYY-MM-DD}` (`lib/funnel.ts`, `lib/analytics.ts`); Pressed Pay and Paid come from orders. Vercel's script (`app/site/SiteAnalytics.tsx`) skips `/admin` and `/account` and strips every query key but the shop's filters, search and `utm_*`, so a receipt's ref and token never leave; the page reads it back through Vercel's query API (`lib/admin/vercel.ts`, `VERCEL_API_TOKEN`, `VERCEL_PROJECT_ID`, `VERCEL_TEAM_ID`), and says how to switch it on when it can't. `/admin/analytics/export?month=` is the month's sales and refunds as CSV for the books.
- Search: the header's search mark (`app/site/HeaderSearch.tsx`) opens a field and a results window, matching product names, category names and tag labels via `lib/catalog/search.ts`; it loads the catalogue once from `/api/search` (static, hourly). "See all" goes to `/shop/all?q=`, which the catalogue filters with the same matcher.
- The admin console is at `/admin` (`app/admin/`): staff sign in at `/admin/login` (`lib/admin/auth.ts`: Firebase Auth from the server, an httpOnly session cookie scoped to `/admin`, and an admin only by the `admin: true` claim or `ADMIN_EMAILS`). Orders (`/admin/orders`, `lib/admin/orders.ts`) lists open, flagged and recent orders; an order's page (`OrderView.tsx`) is a workbench: a read-only stage track (`StageTrack.tsx`, whose only control moves it back a stage), then its next step (`NextStep.tsx`: the one thing to do now, from payment, issues, stage and waybill, with its task done in place: the waybill booking form, resolving issues, accepting a missed PayFast payment, settling an unknown booking; held at the top while scrolling on desktop when it's only a button), then the print sheet (`PrintSheet.tsx`: every submitted file with its print size and shape from `lib/admin/printSpecs.ts`, each with Download, fetched from Cloudinary in the browser with no transformation, and Print, through the print view at `/admin/print/<ref>`), with Print receipt in its header (`/admin/orders/<ref>/receipt`, an A4 PDF on the shop's letterhead drawn with `pdf-lib` in `lib/admin/receipt.ts`, in Archivo and Geist Mono embedded from `lib/admin/fonts` (OFL; `next.config.ts` traces them and the logo into the route): shop, customer, delivery, lines with SKUs, deals, refunds and the PayFast payment; an unpaid order prints as an order summary), with the customer, delivery and money in a glance card beside it, and the courier, payment and every PayFast notice, emails and history in tabs (`RecordTabs.tsx`) under the work; its stage (new, being made, ready, done), the courier (`lib/checkout/shipments.ts`, panel in `app/admin/(console)/orders/[ref]/Courier.tsx`: staff book the waybill once it's packed, choosing the boxes and weights, optionally a collection day and hours and the account's paid extras (`/rates/opt-in`), with TCG's price for exactly that shown against what the customer paid before booking; once delivered, its proof of delivery (read from TCG on request; the links expire) and a return to the shop (`returnShipment`, reference `<ref>-R`), handled by the same functions with a direction; the order is held while TCG is asked, and a booking whose outcome is unknown must be looked up, by the order's reference among TCG's recent shipments, before booking again, since TCG has no idempotency key; then the waybill and stickers PDFs, cancel, and its status, kept by TCG's unsigned webhook at `/api/tcg/webhook?key=` (which only triggers a re-read from TCG) and a daily cron, `/api/cron/track-shipments`; the order moves to Sent once TCG has it, problems are flagged, and booking emails the customer the tracking number), refunds (made in PayFast's dashboard, recorded here), issues to mark as looked at, and its emails to resend, with every change logged by who made it. Payments (`/admin/payments`, `lib/admin/payments.ts`) sets PayFast's own transaction history for a month beside the orders (read live through PayFast's signed API, `lib/checkout/payfastApi.ts`), exceptions first: a payment whose notice never arrived, a wrong amount, a second payment, one naming no order, or a paid order PayFast has no record of. A daily cron (`/api/cron/payfast-check`, 06:00 SAST) and Check now flag each exception on its order once (`orders/{ref}/notices/history-…`); an unpaid order whose payment PayFast's history shows can be accepted from its page (the only way the admin marks an order paid, through the same path as a notice). An order's payment panel also asks PayFast about its card payment and reads its refunds from PayFast; the dashboard shows the last check and PayFast's status page when it reports trouble. Products is a table with bulk status and special changes, and an editor (`app/admin/editor/`) with tabs for details, prices, photos, customer photo and filters, a live preview, a save bar, a leave guard and Duplicate. Every write is a server action in `app/admin/actions.ts` that checks the session itself; storage is `lib/admin/products.ts` (reads fresh, not cached) and the rules shared with the browser are `lib/catalog/edit.ts`. Saving refreshes the shop through the `catalog` cache tag. Product photos upload from the browser to Cloudinary, signed by `lib/admin/cloudinary.ts`, and photos staff remove are destroyed there.
- If you build backend logic, run /simplify after implementing.
- This is an e-commerce website, all implemented logic should reflect that of the industry-standard.

## Stack

- Next.js 16 (App Router, React 19), TypeScript. Read the bundled Next docs before writing code (see AGENTS.md).
- Mantine v9 is the primary UI library, with Tailwind v4 alongside it for layout. Mantine docs: https://mantine.dev/llms.txt
- GSAP for motion.
- Firebase (Firestore) holds the product catalogue and orders; Firebase Auth signs in staff and customers (server side only). Payments go through PayFast.
- `package.json` pins `jwks-rsa` to 3.2.2 through `overrides`. `firebase-admin` 14 asks for `jwks-rsa` 4, whose `jose` 6 is ESM-only and loaded with `require()`; Vercel's Node 24 runtime refuses that (`ERR_REQUIRE_ESM`), so every page that loads `firebase-admin/auth` fails with a 500 there, though it works locally. `jwks-rsa` 3.2.2 uses `jose` 4, which ships CommonJS, and `firebase-admin` uses only its unchanged client (for App Check and phone-number tokens; sessions don't touch it). The override wins over whatever a newer `firebase-admin` asks for: when upgrading it, check what it needs, and remove the override only once loading works with `node --no-experimental-require-module node_modules/next/dist/bin/next start` (which reproduces Vercel's behaviour).

## Layout

- `app/page.tsx` — the homepage; styles in `app/home.css` and `app/globals.css`.
- `app/site/` — shared chrome (`SiteHeader`, `SiteFooter`, `OrderDock`, `chrome.css`) and homepage components (`Carousel`, `CategoryCards`, `Newsletter`, `Motion`, `icons`).
- `app/shop/` — the storefront and catalogue; `shelves.ts` resolves shelves and the rail from the catalogue, `lib/catalog/listing.ts` holds card data, price formatting, sorts and price bands.

- `public/product_list.json` — the original catalogue export; superseded by Firestore.

## Catalogue (Firebase)

- Firestore project `expressphoto-c94ab`. Schema and its conventions live in `lib/catalog/types.ts`: `categories/{id}` and `products/{slug}` with embedded options, variants (SKU + price in ZAR cents) and media (`role: "photo" | "dimension"` — dimension diagrams appear only on the detailed product page).
- Five categories (`photo-gifts`, `frames`, `photo-printing`, `jewellery`, `add-ons`); finer grouping is tags, not sub-categories. Tags are `facet:value` from the closed vocabulary in `lib/catalog/tags.ts`: `type`, `occasion` and `for` are shopper filters (in the URL as `?type=&occasion=&for=`); `process`, `fits` and `holds` are internal, and a `fits` meeting a `holds` is a "Goes with this" pairing (`lib/catalog/related.ts`). Old category URLs redirect in `next.config.ts`.
- The admin console is where the catalogue is edited, including what the customer supplies (`personalisation`) and tags; specials are campaigns. `scripts/catalog-merchandising.mts` only seeds personalisation and tags for the importer, which fails on a product missing from it and never writes `special`.
- Read it only on the server through `lib/catalog/queries.ts` (Admin SDK, cached under the `catalog` tag). `firestore.rules` denies all browser access; deploy changes with `firebase deploy --only firestore:rules`.
- `npm run import:catalog` seeds Firestore from `Express Photo Website Catalogue.xlsx` and refuses to write over a catalogue that already has products; `-- --replace` replaces it, discarding every admin edit, and `-- --dry-run` writes `catalog-preview.json` instead. Prices come from the "Product Catalogue" sheet, text from the "Quick List" sheet; products without a price import as `draft` and are not shown. Print-process prefixes ("Sublimation") are stripped from names and kept in `tags`.

## Images (Cloudinary)

- Product photos live in Cloudinary under `products/<code>`, not in `public/`. Render them with `ProductImage` (`app/site/ProductImage.tsx`), which uses `next-cloudinary` with a fixed set of widths.
- Credentials are in `.env.local` (`NEXT_PUBLIC_CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLOUDINARY_API_KEY`, `CLOUDINARY_API_SECRET`). Never expose the secret to client code.
- Cloudinary is on the free tier and must stay there. Keep storage, bandwidth and transformation usage to a minimum: store assets no larger than they are displayed, deliver with f_auto/q_auto at the smallest width that renders sharply, and limit the number of distinct derived sizes (add to the shared width list in `ProductImage` rather than inventing new ones).

## Mobile text size

Text smaller than 16px on form inputs, textareas and select menus makes mobile Safari zoom in when tapped. No text element may be smaller than 16px on mobile.
